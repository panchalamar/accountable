# Accountable MVP: panchalamar

A sales CRM for one person, running locally: a dashboard with a revenue and forecast chart, a sales funnel and follow-ups, then organizations, contacts, deals with activity timelines, and a drag-and-drop pipeline board. FastAPI, SQLite and Alembic on the back end, React, Vite and Tailwind on the front end, with Vitest, pytest and Playwright behind one `npm test`.

These are the documents I wrote on Week 1 Day 3:

- `intent.md`: the intent, after two interviews
- `spec.md`: concerns, requirements, architecture, data model, API, screens, visual design and the chosen design
- `plan.md`: seven build phases, risks, and the tests that prove the spec
- `REVIEW.md`: the review policy

The design is mockup 7, which combines the forecast chart, funnel and dark side column of earlier mockups with an arrow-headed pipeline board. The mockups and their screenshots are in [docs/mockups](https://github.com/panchalamar/accountable/tree/main/docs/mockups).

The full build is in my fork: https://github.com/panchalamar/accountable
